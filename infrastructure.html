Infrastructure in India

1 Introduction

Infrastructure provides the supporting services in the main areas of industrial and agricultural production, domestic and foreign trade, and commerce. It is divided into two categories:

  • Economic Infrastructure: Energy, Transport, and Communication. Directly links to the economy.
  • Social Infrastructure: Education, Health, and Housing. Improves the quality of human resources.

2 Energy Sector

Energy is a critical aspect of the development process. India is transitioning towards renewable energy to meet its Panchamrit targets set at COP26.

Key Note for Prelims:

India's target is 500 GW of non-fossil fuel capacity by 2030.

4 Public-Private Partnership (PPP) Models

Since the government alone cannot fund the massive infrastructure deficit, PPP models are crucial. Key models include:

BOT (Build-Operate-Transfer)

Private entity builds, operates to recover cost, and then transfers to the govt.

HAM (Hybrid Annuity Model)

Mix of EPC (40% govt funding) and BOT (60% private). Lowers risk for private players.
