Infrastructure in India

1 Introduction

Infrastructure provides the supporting services in the main areas of industrial and agricultural production, domestic and foreign trade, and commerce.

  • Economic Infrastructure: Energy, Transport, and Communication. Directly links to the economy.
  • Social Infrastructure: Education, Health, and Housing. Improves the quality of human resources.
