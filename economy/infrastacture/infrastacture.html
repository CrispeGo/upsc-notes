Infrastructure in India

The backbone of economic and social development — understanding what drives India's growth engine.

1 Introduction

Infrastructure provides the supporting services in the main areas of industrial and agricultural production, domestic and foreign trade, and commerce.

💡

Key Idea: Infrastructure is not a direct productive sector — it enables and supports all other sectors. Think of it as the "foundation" on which the economy is built.

⚡ Economic Infrastructure

Energy, Transport, and Communication. Directly and immediately linked to the productive processes of the economy — enables production and trade.

🏫 Social Infrastructure

Education, Health, and Housing. Improves the quality and productivity of human resources — an indirect but equally vital contributor to growth.
